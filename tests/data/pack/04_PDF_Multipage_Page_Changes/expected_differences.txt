TEST 04 - MULTI-PAGE PDF COMPARISON
Compare original_agreement.pdf with revised_agreement.pdf.

Expected differences:
1. Original document has 2 pages; revised document has 3 pages.
2. A new page 3 titled APPENDIX A was inserted. It contains three escalation contact email addresses and a note that the appendix is newly included.
3. On the service schedule page, approval role changed: Service Manager -> Operations Manager.
4. A new charge was added: Emergency on-site visit - Rs. 1,500 per visit.
5. Header accent colour on the revised service schedule / appendix differs from the original.

Expected unchanged items: agreement ID, provider, customer, service description, start date, term, included visits, response target, replacement-parts note, support email, and support hours.

Manual note: Page alignment may report the appended page as an insertion. Confirm it is not incorrectly reported as a modification of an existing page.
